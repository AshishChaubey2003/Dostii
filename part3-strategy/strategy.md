## 1. Experimentation Matrix (A/B Test Variations)

3 alag variations test karenge is ad ki:

---

### Variation 1 - Hook Change (Sad to Funny)

| Element   | Original                          | V1 Change                              |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Hook      | "Raat ke 2 baj rahe hain..." sad  | "Phir akele dosa khaya?" funny/relatable |
| Tone      | Emotional, melancholic            | Light, humorous                        |
| Music     | Sad instrumental                  | Upbeat desi beat                       |
| Target    | Emotionally tired drivers         | Drivers who like comedy content        |

**Hypothesis:** Funny hook pe CTR zyada hoga kyunki log sad content skip karte hain.

---

### Variation 2 - Female Voice Profile Change

| Element      | Original                    | V2 Change                        |
|--------------|-----------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Female Voice | Caring, soft, Hindi         | Energetic, fun, Hinglish          |
| Dialogue     | "Haan bolo, kaisi rahi drive?" | "Arre Raju bhai! Kya scene hai?" |
| Vibe         | Emotional support            | Fun friendship                   |
| Target       | Lonely, emotionally drained  | Bored, wants entertainment       |

**Hypothesis:** Energetic voice pe Install Rate zyada hogi kyunki fun lagega.

---

### Variation 3 - Background Music Change

| Element | Original                  | V3 Change                     |
|---------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Music   | Sad Hindi instrumental     | Popular Haryanvi folk beat    |
| Feel    | Bollywood emotional        | Regional, desi, familiar      |
| Connect | General Hindi audience     | Specifically Haryana/UP drivers|

**Hypothesis:** Regional music se specific audience zyada connect karegi aur CAC kam hoga.

## 2. Tracking & ROAS - First 7 Days Metrics

### Primary Metrics (Sabse Important)

| Metric              | Target        | Why Important                              |
|---------------------|---------------|--------------------------------------------|
| CTR (Click Rate)    | 2% - 4%       | Ad dekh ke click kiya ya nahi              |
| Install Rate        | 15% - 25%     | Click ke baad app download kiya ya nahi    |
| Cost Per Install    | Rs 20 - Rs 40 | Ek install pe kitna paisa laga             |
| Day 1 Retention     | 40%+          | Download ke baad kal bhi aaya ya nahi      |

### Secondary Metrics

| Metric              | Target   | Why Important                          |
|---------------------|----------|----------------------------------------|
| Video View Rate     | 50%+     | Kitne logo ne pura video dekha          |
| Hook Retention      | 70%+     | Pehle 5 second ke baad kitne ruke      |
| ROAS               | 2x+      | Rs 100 lagaye, Rs 200+ revenue aaya     |
| CPM                 | Rs 40-80 | 1000 impressions ka cost               |

### Day-by-Day Plan

| Day   | Action                                              |
|-------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Day 1 | Ad launch, baseline CTR dekho                       |
| Day 2 | Hook retention check - 5 second drop-off rate       |
| Day 3 | Install Rate vs Click Rate compare karo             |
| Day 4 | Worst performing variation band karo                |
| Day 5 | Budget best variation pe double karo                |
| Day 6 | Day 1 retention dekho - kya log wapas aa rahe hain  |
| Day 7 | Full ROAS calculate karo, decision lo               |

## 3. CAC Optimization - Agar Cost Zyada Ho

### Problem: Truck Driver Segment mein CAC zyada hai

### Solution 1 - Creative Format Badlo

```
Current:  15-second horizontal video
Change:   6-second vertical bumper ad (YouTube)

Reason:   Chhota ad = less skip = better completion rate = lower CAC
```

### Solution 2 - Prompt/Hook Badlo

```
Current:  Generic sad hook
Change:   Hyper-specific hook - "NH-44 pe akele ho?"

Reason:   Driver apna route sunta hai toh instantly relate karta hai
          Relevance badhti hai = CTR badhta hai = CAC kam hota hai
```

### Solution 3 - Platform Shift Karo

```
Current:  YouTube pre-roll ads
Change:   Moj / Josh in-feed ads

Reason:   Moj/Josh pe truck driver audience zyada concentrated hai
          Competition kam hai = CPM kam = CAC kam
```

### Solution 4 - Time Targeting

```
Current:  All day ads
Change:   Only 9 PM - 2 AM ads

Reason:   Truck drivers is time pe parked hote hain aur phone use karte hain
          Relevant time pe dikhao = better conversion = lower CAC
```

### CAC Optimization Formula

```
CAC kam karne ka order:
1. Pehle creative fix karo (hook, duration)
2. Phir platform fix karo (Moj/Josh)
3. Phir timing fix karo (night only)
4. Last mein targeting fix karo (NH routes)
```
