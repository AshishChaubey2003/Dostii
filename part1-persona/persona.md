## Pain Points (Dard Kya Hai?)

1. Akela pan - 20+ nights/month akele highway pe, koi baat karne wala nahi
2. Ghar ki yaad - Wife/family ke calls short hote hain, sirf "paise bheje?" tak
3. Road Fatigue - Same road, same silence, har raat
4. Koi sunne wala nahi - Emotional stress badhta rehta hai, outlet nahi
5. Social Disconnect - Gaon ke dost alag ho gaye, naye dost nahi bane

## Why This Persona?

- India mein 3.5 crore+ truck drivers hain
- 20+ raatein/month ghar se door rehte hain
- Raat 10 baje se 2 baje tak phone heavily use karte hain
- YouTube, Moj, Josh pe milte hain
- Hindi content se deeply connect karte hain
- CAC kam hai kyunki is segment mein competition low hai
