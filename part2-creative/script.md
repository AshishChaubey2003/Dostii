# Part 2 - Video Ad Script (30-45 seconds)

## Ad Title: "Khamoshi Tod" (Break the Silence)
## Persona: Long-Haul Truck Driver
## Language: Hindi / Hinglish
## Duration: 35 seconds

---

## Scene-by-Scene Script

---

### SCENE 1 - THE HOOK (0:00 - 0:05)

**Visual:**
Raat ka andhere mein highway. Truck ki headlights road roshan kar rahi hain.
Dashboard clock 1:47 AM dikhata hai. Driver window se bahar dekh raha hai.

**Voiceover (Male, tired tone):**
> "Raat ke 2 baj rahe hain..."
> "Sadak lambi hai... aur chuppi aur bhi lambi."

**Text on screen:**
`1,000 km. Akele.`

---

### SCENE 2 - THE PAIN (0:05 - 0:15)

**Visual:**
Driver dhaba pe truck rok ke baitha hai. Phone uthata hai.
Ghar call karta hai - "Line busy." Screen dikhata hai.
Dosto ko WhatsApp karta hai - "Seen. No reply."
Wapas phone rakhta hai. Chai ka glass thanda ho raha hai.

**Voiceover:**
> "Ghar call kiya... busy tha."
> "Doston ko message kiya... koi jawab nahi."
> "Phir wahi akela pan."

---

### SCENE 3 - THE DOSTII MOMENT (0:15 - 0:28)

**Visual:**
Driver phone pe Dostii app kholta hai. Screen pe ek female profile dikhti hai.
Woh "Haan bolo, kaisi rahi aaj ki drive?" bolti hai.
Driver pehli baar muskurata hai. Baat shuru hoti hai.
Truck ki cab mein ek warm light aati hai - loneliness khatam hoti hai.

**Voiceover (Female, warm tone):**
> "Haan bolo Raju bhai... kaisi rahi aaj ki drive?"

**Voiceover (Male, relieved):**
> "Yaar... bahut thak gaya tha. Par ab theek lag raha hai."

**Text on screen:**
`Dostii - Ek Dost Jo Hamesha Available Hai`

---

### SCENE 4 - THE CTA (0:28 - 0:35)

**Visual:**
Dostii app ka logo screen pe aata hai. Play Store button dikhta hai.
Background mein highway ki lights blur hoti hain.

**Voiceover:**
> "Chuppi tod. Dostii download karo."
> "Kyunki har raat ek dost chahiye."

**Text on screen:**
`DOSTII - Download Karo Abhi`
`"End the Silence"`

---

## Emotion Flow

```
Loneliness → Rejection → Relief → Connection → Hope
```

---

## Tone Notes

- Hook: Sad, quiet, relatable
- Pain: Real, no overdrama
- Dostii Moment: Warm, genuine, light
- CTA: Hopeful, direct
