# Video Making Instructions - Step by Step

## Agar Video Khud Banana Ho Toh Ye Steps Follow Karo

---

## Step 1 - Pika Labs Se Video Clips Banao

1. pika.art pe jao, free account banao
2. "Generate Video" click karo
3. prompt-sheet.md se Scene 1 ka prompt copy karo
4. Paste karo, Generate karo
5. Download karo (Scene1.mp4)
6. Yahi step 4 scenes ke liye repeat karo

**Expected output:** 4 clips of 5-10 seconds each

---

## Step 2 - ElevenLabs Se Voiceover Banao

1. elevenlabs.io pe jao, free account banao
2. "Text to Speech" section mein jao
3. prompt-sheet.md se male voiceover text copy karo
4. Hindi voice select karo
5. Generate karo, download karo (voice_male.mp3)
6. Yahi female voice ke liye karo (voice_female.mp3)

---

## Step 3 - Suno AI Se Music Banao

1. suno.com pe jao, free account banao
2. prompt-sheet.md ka music prompt paste karo
3. Generate karo
4. Best option download karo (background_music.mp3)

---

## Step 4 - CapCut Mein Sab Jodo

1. CapCut app ya website pe jao
2. "New Project" banao, 9:16 (vertical) ya 16:9 (horizontal) select karo
3. Scene1.mp4, Scene2.mp4, Scene3.mp4, Scene4.mp4 import karo
4. Order mein arrange karo timeline pe
5. voice_male.mp3 add karo as audio track
6. voice_female.mp3 add karo at 15 second mark
7. background_music.mp3 add karo, volume 25-30% rakho
8. Hindi text overlays add karo:
   - 0:00 - "Raat ke 2 baj rahe hain..."
   - 0:05 - "Ghar call kiya... busy tha"
   - 0:15 - "Dostii - Ek Dost Jo Hamesha Available Hai"
   - 0:28 - "DOSTII - Download Karo Abhi"
9. Export karo: 1080p MP4, 35 seconds

---

## Final Output

- File name: final-ad.mp4
- Duration: 35 seconds
- Resolution: 1080p
- Format: MP4
